About Smart Disaster Management

An intelligent disaster management platform designed to improve disaster awareness, emergency response and public safety through technology and artificial intelligence.

What is Smart Disaster Management System?

Smart Disaster Management System is a web-based platform that brings important disaster management services into one place. It helps users monitor weather conditions, receive disaster alerts, report disasters, find emergency shelters, request SOS assistance and participate as volunteers.

🤖 AI-Powered Disaster Risk Prediction

The system uses machine learning to analyze important weather parameters such as temperature, humidity, wind speed, rainfall and atmospheric pressure. Based on these conditions, the system predicts disaster risk as LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH.


When a high disaster risk is detected, the system can automatically generate a disaster alert to help users take necessary precautions.

System Features

🚨 Disaster Alerts

Provides important disaster warnings and emergency notifications to users.

🌦️ Weather Monitoring

Shows current weather information and forecast data for better disaster awareness.

🗺️ Live Map

Displays disaster locations, shelters and useful emergency information on an interactive map.

🆘 SOS Emergency

Allows users to request emergency assistance during dangerous situations.

🏠 Emergency Shelters

Helps users find available shelters and emergency accommodation locations.

🤝 Volunteers

Supports volunteer registration and disaster response activities.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a centralized and intelligent disaster management platform that improves preparedness, awareness, communication and emergency response during natural disasters.
